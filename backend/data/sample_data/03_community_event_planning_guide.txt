# Community Event Planning & Logistics Guide (Illustrative Reference)
Source Label: Curated EventWise Community Guide (Illustrative Estimates)
Location Scope: General India
Event Type: community

## 1. Community Event Essentials
Community festivals, neighborhood cultural evenings, Resident Welfare Association (RWA) gatherings, and college symposiums require transparent budgeting, crowd safety, and volunteer coordination. All monetary examples are illustrative estimates in INR.

## 2. Illustrative Community Budget Allocation
- Venue and permits: 20% — Community hall booking, shamiana canopy rental, seating chairs, and local permissions.
- Food and beverages: 32% — Bottled/filtered drinking water stations, tea/coffee counter, and boxed or buffet community meal.
- Stage, sound and AV: 16% — PA speakers, cordless microphones, projector/screen, and podium lighting.
- Decoration and signage: 10% — Cloth banners, registration desk signage, badges, and stage floral pots.
- Miscellaneous and logistics: 10% — First-aid kit, waste segregation bins, volunteer refreshments, and cleaning staff.
- Emergency reserve: 12% — Contingency reserve for weather protection or extra seating.

## 3. Crowd Safety & Sustainability Checklist
- Place separate wet and dry waste bins every 25 meters near food counters.
- Maintain clear entry and exit aisles of at least 2 meters width for emergency access.
- Assign 1 volunteer steward for every 25–30 expected attendees.
- Test PA system and backup inverter 24 hours prior to the gathering.
