# Vendor Evaluation & Booking Verification Checklist
Source Label: Curated EventWise Vendor Due-Diligence Guide
Location Scope: General India
Event Type: general

## 1. Important Disclaimer
EventWise AI operates strictly offline/local-first and never provides live vendor availability, verified market prices, or booking confirmations. Users must independently verify every vendor quote in writing.

## 2. Pre-Booking Evaluation Questions
- Venue Checklist:
  - What is the exact hall access window (setup time + event hours + teardown)?
  - Is Diesel Generator (DG) power backup included in the base rental or billed per hour?
  - Are outside decorators and caterers permitted without corkage or royalty fees?
  - How many car and two-wheeler parking slots are available on-site?
- Catering Checklist:
  - Does the per-plate illustrative quote include service staff, chafing fuel, drinking water cans, and banana leaves/crockery?
  - What is the cut-off date for increasing or reducing the guaranteed guest count?
- Decoration & Photography Checklist:
  - Request real photos of past setups in similar hall sizes.
  - Specify exact flower types, lighting count, and album deliverable timelines in the written estimate.
- Payment Milestone Rule:
  - Never pay 100% upfront. Follow a staged structure: 25% booking advance, 50% one week before the event, and 25% upon completion on event day.
