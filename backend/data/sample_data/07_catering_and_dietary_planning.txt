# Catering & Dietary Menu Planning Guide (Illustrative Estimates)
Source Label: Curated EventWise Catering Reference
Location Scope: Chennai & General India
Event Type: general

## 1. Menu Structuring & Headcount Buffers
Food and beverages typically account for 30% to 35% of an event's total budget. Always confirm a 5% to 10% plate buffer with your caterer to handle unexpected guests smoothly. All per-plate figures below are illustrative synthetic estimates in INR, not verified caterer quotes.

## 2. Pure Vegetarian & Specialty Dietary Planning
- Pure Vegetarian Buffet (Illustrative ₹150–₹350 per plate for casual events; ₹450–₹800 for weddings):
  - Welcome Drink: Fresh lime mint cooler or buttermilk (neer mor) / badam milk.
  - Starters: Paneer tikka, crispy corn, or medu vada with chutneys.
  - Mains: 1 rich paneer/mushroom gravy, 1 seasonal vegetable subzi, dal tadka/sambar, assorted rotis/pooris, and vegetable pulao or biryani.
  - Desserts: Gulab jamun with ice cream or traditional payasam.
- Jain & Allergen-Friendly Adaptations:
  - Set up a clearly labeled No-Onion No-Garlic (Jain) counter and list nut/dairy allergens on buffet cards.
  - Use separate serving spoons for vegan, Jain, and gluten-free dishes.
- Live Counters:
  - Dosa/chaat live counters keep food hot and reduce wastage compared to pre-cooked chafing dishes.
