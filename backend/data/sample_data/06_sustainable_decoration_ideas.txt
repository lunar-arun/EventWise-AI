# Sustainable & Budget-Friendly Decoration Ideas (Illustrative Guide)
Source Label: Curated EventWise Decor Studio Guide
Location Scope: General India
Event Type: general

## 1. Cost-Effective Decoration Techniques
Decoration often overruns budgets when planners rely on single-use foam cutouts and imported flowers. Use these high-impact, budget-friendly alternatives (all costs are illustrative INR estimates):
- Reusable Fabric & Fairy Lights Backdrop (Illustrative ₹1,500–₹3,500): Combine pastel or royal navy cotton/satin drapes with warm 300-LED curtain lights.
- Seasonal Local Florals (Illustrative ₹800–₹2,500): Use locally sourced marigold, jasmine, chrysanthemum, and mango leaf torans for vibrant cultural warmth at a fraction of imported orchid costs.
- Potted Plant Rentals (Illustrative ₹1,000–₹2,000): Rent indoor palms, ferns, and terracotta pots from a local nursery to flank the stage and entrance.
- DIY Photo Booth Corner (Illustrative ₹500–₹1,200): Create a themed frame with cardboard props, chalkboard quotes, and ring light illumination.

## 2. Theme Color Combinations
- Star Kingdom Retro Theme: Sky Blue (#8EDBFA), Mario Red (#E8483F), Pipe Green (#279B52), Coin Gold (#FFC928), and Deep Navy (#20304A).
- Royal South Indian Heritage: Temple Gold, Crimson Silk, Banana Leaf Green, and Ivory.
- Sunset Garden Party: Terracotta, Marigold Yellow, Sage Green, and Warm Cream.
