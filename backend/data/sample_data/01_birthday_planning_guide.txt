# Birthday Party Planning Guidance (Illustrative Reference Guide)
Source Label: Curated EventWise Birthday Guide (Synthetic & Illustrative Estimates)
Location Scope: Chennai & General India
Event Type: birthday

## 1. Overview & Planning Philosophy
Planning a memorable birthday celebration requires balancing guest comfort, engaging entertainment, and disciplined budget management. All monetary figures in this document are illustrative synthetic estimates in Indian Rupees (INR), not verified market research or live vendor quotes.

## 2. Illustrative ₹25,000 Birthday Budget Breakdown (50 Guests)
For an illustrative birthday budget of ₹25,000 INR, recommended category allocations are:
- Venue: ₹5,000 (20%) — Community party hall, terrace venue, or restaurant mini-hall rental for 4 hours.
- Food and beverages: ₹8,000 (32%) — Welcome drink, 2 starters, main course buffet, and ice cream (~₹140–₹160 per plate illustrative estimate).
- Decoration: ₹3,000 (12%) — Balloon arch, reusable fabric backdrop, warm LED fairy lights, and welcome board.
- Cake: ₹2,000 (8%) — 2 kg custom theme celebration cake with celebratory toppers.
- Entertainment: ₹2,000 (8%) — Portable PA speaker rental, curated game props, and DIY photo booth accessories.
- Miscellaneous: ₹2,000 (8%) — Digital invitations, eco-friendly return gifts, and disposable biodegradable plates.
- Emergency reserve: ₹3,000 (12%) — Protected buffer for 5–8 extra guests or last-minute transport needs.
Total: ₹25,000 INR.

## 3. Recommended Birthday Themes
- Retro Starlight Adventure: Gold stars, sky-blue drapes, coin-gold balloon clusters, and pixel-art milestone signs.
- Tropical Garden Fiesta: Potted local palms, marigold garlands, terracotta lamps, and fruit punch counter.
- Pastel Cloud Kingdom: Soft white and sky-blue balloon clouds, warm Edison bulbs, and acoustic music.

## 4. Key Birthday Planning Milestones
- Three weeks before: Finalize total INR budget, lock the ₹3,000 emergency reserve, and list invited guests.
- Two weeks before: Visit 2–3 local venues and catering providers; verify backup power and parking.
- One week before: Send digital invitations, confirm RSVP headcount, and finalize cake design and flavor.
- Three days before: Verify decoration supplies, return gift bags, and playlists.
- One day before: Complete packing of candles, cake knife, matches, first-aid kit, and UPI/cash envelope.
- Event day: Arrive 90 minutes early to inspect seating, air-conditioning, food warmth, and photo backdrop.
