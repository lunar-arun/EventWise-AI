# Wedding Preparation Master Checklist (Illustrative Reference Guide)
Source Label: Curated EventWise Wedding Handbook (Illustrative Estimates)
Location Scope: South India / General India
Event Type: wedding

## 1. Wedding Planning Structure
Weddings involve multi-stage coordination across venue booking, traditional rituals, multi-course catering, mandap decoration, and guest hospitality. All cost ranges below are illustrative estimates in INR, not verified vendor quotes.

## 2. Illustrative Wedding Budget Ratios
- Venue & Marriage Hall (Kalyana Mandapam / Lawn): 24% of total budget (includes generator backup, dining hall, and bride/groom rooms).
- Food and Beverages (Wedding Sadhya / Buffet Feast): 34% of total budget (includes welcome beverages, live tiffin counters, main leaf/buffet meal, and desserts).
- Decoration & Floral Mandap: 14% of total budget (stage backdrop, fresh jasmine/marigold garlands, entrance arch, and pathway lighting).
- Photography & Videography: 8% of total budget (traditional + candid coverage and digital album).
- Entertainment & Ritual Supplies: 5% of total budget (Nadaswaram/melam or acoustic ensemble, sound system, and ceremonial items).
- Miscellaneous & Hospitality: 5% of total budget (return tambulam bags, welcome signage, and local guest shuttle).
- Emergency Reserve: 10% of total budget (protected buffer for extra plates, weather canopies, or overtime hall charges).

## 3. Chronological Wedding Milestones
- Two months before: Finalize total wedding budget, guest list (e.g., 150–300 guests), and book marriage hall and primary caterer.
- One month before: Complete menu tasting (including pure vegetarian, Jain, or regional specialties) and book photography team.
- Two weeks before: Dispatch invitations, finalize floral mandap design, and confirm guest room bookings.
- One week before: Reconfirm vendor arrival times, electrical load requirements, and seating layout.
- One day before: Inspect venue cleanliness, dressing rooms, stage safety, and hospitality desk.
- Event day: Follow the ceremony and dining timeline with a dedicated family floor coordinator.
